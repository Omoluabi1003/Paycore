# Visible payroll operations workspace

The home screen now starts with the payroll command center rather than the editable grid and task tiles. It shows the chosen working period, calculated-row net/revenue/earnings/deductions/reimbursements/adjustments, captured work entries, ready/review/blocked counts, calculation-readiness percentage, visible exceptions and direct actions. An empty payroll stays at zero and asks the specialist to add contractors; no sample roster is inserted. Readiness means freedom from calculation issues, not production launch readiness or human approval.

The sidebar adds Contractors, Activity register and Exceptions. Contractor 360 has working Overview, Activity, Pay terms, Balances and Statement sections built from entered facts and deterministic lines. Search/status filtering affects the workbench; the activity register searches contractor/reference/type/description; exceptions link to editing or guided review. Quick Add contractor creates a deliberate new draft row and focuses it. Add load/expense opens the corresponding entry form. No UI-only click is described as a live integration.

Incomplete/duplicate rows count as blocked and have no projected money. Financial totals are summed with BigInt and safe-integer checks. Reviewed demo snapshots drive post-approval views. Existing draft entry, guide, warning acknowledgements, advance plans, frozen simulated approval/paid/lock, and local entered-data exports remain available. Editing moves to the Run payroll view; home remains a summary. Schedule details are collapsible, and the arrival splash is hidden throughout an active workspace so it cannot cover operations after focus changes.

Demo boundaries remain visible. These are in-tab records and simulations; refresh/sign-out clears figures. No cutoff or payment date is invented, and no production identity, persistent audit or real payment connection is claimed. Dates, historical drafts and immutable demo snapshots retain their existing behavior.

Validation includes empty/incomplete/duplicate/readiness/rate/negative-net/frozen-snapshot domain tests; full suite, TypeScript and production build; Chromium navigation/search/profile/activity/exception/direct-entry and complete simulated payroll/download regression; desktop/mobile screenshots and mobile overflow check. No database migration or new environment settings. Rollback by reverting the command-center/read-model/navigation and styling changes; no financial schema or authoritative record is changed.
