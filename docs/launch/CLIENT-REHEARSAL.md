# Client exhibit rehearsal

All identities, agreements, receipts and amounts below are independently invented synthetic test data. This is an exhibition rehearsal, not workbook parity certification or a production payroll run.

## Walkthrough

1. Open the workspace and enter the first working period explicitly. Confirm those dates remain selected.
2. Start presentation and open contractor setup. Enter a fictional contractor, an explicit compensation rate, described recurring insurance and an advance plan; verify and activate.
3. Enter revenue or individual loads. Inspect the automatically calculated earnings and obligations.
4. Enter separate described fuel receipts. Compare the source total; a mismatch must block approval. Inspect a receipt, correct it with a reason and verify readiness recalculates. A verified receipt-reference correction must clear a duplicate warning without deleting activity.
5. Complete earnings and deduction checks. Review the calculation explanation and statements. The presentation guide must not tick checks or approve anything.
6. Simulate approval explicitly. Verify figures freeze and downloads become available only at this final stage.
7. Preview the contractor's selected statement. Download the PDF; check descriptions, references, balances, YTD and ETL GIS Consulting LLC attribution.
8. Open the next period. Agreements and remaining balances must remain; new loads, receipts and one-time charges must start blank. Compare with the prior frozen statement.
9. Repeat through advance payoff. Return to the original period and confirm its statement is unchanged.
10. Save and restore a fictional draft in the same tab/profile. Verify correction evidence and re-review requirements remain.
11. Open reset. Cancel and Escape must preserve work. Confirmed reset must clear only this exhibit/profile's copies, optionally preserve the schedule, and leave unrelated storage and downloaded files alone.

## Financial example

The synthetic journey uses 70% of $1,000 revenue per period, $25 recurring insurance, a $150 opening advance and a $100 installment limit.

| Exhibit period | Earnings | Insurance | Advance recovery | Remaining advance | Net settlement |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| First | $700 | $25 | $100 | $50 | $575 |
| Second | $700 | $25 | $50 | $0 | $625 |
| Third | $700 | $25 | $0 | $0 | $675 |

Approved-history YTD net is $1,875. The period comparison attributes each $50 increase to the smaller advance deduction. This is an entered synthetic agreement, not a default rate or inferred client policy. No funds move.
